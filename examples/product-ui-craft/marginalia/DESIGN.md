# Marginalia

A magazine editor checks a proposed citation against the source while reading a draft. The passage and the agent’s source note must remain visible together. Selecting Insert citation adds a footnote at the passage and offers undo.

Use a white reading surface, ink (#302338), plum (#342138) for navigation and primary actions, lavender (#f1edf4) for the inspector. Set the article in Georgia/Baskerville and the controls in system sans. Use 1px rules, 4px corners, 40px buttons, and a 4/8/12/16/24/32/48px spacing scale. On a phone, put source notes directly after the article and collapse the navigation into a compact masthead.
