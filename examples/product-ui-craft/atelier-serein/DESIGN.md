# Serein Research

Readers come here to understand what a small machine-perception lab studies and choose a paper. The opening pairs one question about machine vision with one optical experiment. Below it, readers can distinguish a research paper from a field note before opening either.

Use the editorial hierarchy of a research institution. Set the hero in Instrument Serif, keep the navigation in the system sans, and set plate captions in a system monospace. Warm paper `#f8f5ee`, ink `#24231e`, secondary ink `#59574e`, rule `#cbc6ba` and oxide `#a4422f` frame a single coral lithograph. Flat printed surfaces, a one-pixel rule, a circular optical mark and two small experimental plates carry the identity.

Use a 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64px spacing scale, 40/48px desktop controls and a 44px mobile target. Body type is 17–20px, captions 12–14px, the display heading 64–112px. Desktop uses two columns. On a phone the text precedes the plate and publications become a list. Use native links, buttons and a modal dialog with Escape and visible keyboard focus. The light paper colour is part of the artwork in either system theme.

The publication texts and the lab are fictional. The lens illustration was made with the built-in ImageGen tool. Its art direction was a single upright convex lens bending parallel dark rays on a full-bleed apricot ground, rendered as a cream-and-black halftone lithograph. The two publication thumbnails are generated optical test patterns. Instrument Serif is distributed with its SIL Open Font License in `OFL.txt`.
