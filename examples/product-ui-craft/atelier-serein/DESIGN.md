# Atelier Serein

Potential clients browse a small practice’s work. Their first action is to open a project. A monumental serif masthead gives way to one panoramic architectural photograph, followed by its project notes.

Warm paper (#f4f1eb), espresso ink (#241f19), ochre concrete from the artwork. Use Baskerville for the masthead and project title, a system sans for navigation, Menlo for field labels. Use 1px rules, square corners, 40px controls, and a 4/8/16/24/32/48px spacing scale. On narrow screens, stack the practice introduction under the masthead.
